About Us

Green Acres is a 25-acre organic farm run by the same family for three generations. We grow vegetables, mangoes, sugarcane and millets, and keep cows, goats, and free-range poultry. Our mission is simple: help visitors understand where food comes from while supporting local farmers and rural communities.

Why Visit Us?

  • 100% organic farming practices
  • Farm-to-table meals made from local produce
  • Safe, family-friendly activities for all ages
  • Income goes back to farm families and village artisans
  • Just 90 minutes from the city

Things To Do

Fruit and Vegetable Picking

Harvest seasonal produce yourself and take home what you pick.

Milking and Animal Feeding

Meet our cows, goats, rabbits and chickens. Children love feeding time.

Bullock Cart and Tractor Rides

Tour the fields on a traditional bullock cart or a farm tractor.

Cooking Class

Learn to cook regional dishes on a traditional clay stove with our farm cooks.

Nature Walk and Bird Watching

Guided morning walks through orchards, ponds and meadows.

Pottery and Village Crafts

Try your hand at pottery, weaving and basket making with local artisans.

Campfire and Stargazing

End the day with folk music, a bonfire and a clear night sky.

What Is Happening When?

  1. June to September (Monsoon): Paddy transplanting, lush green views, mushroom picking
  2. October to November (Autumn): Harvest festival, sugarcane crushing
  3. December to February (Winter): Jaggery making, bonfire evenings, vegetable harvest
  4. March to May (Summer): Mango picking, pond swimming, early morning walks

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, closed shoes, a hat, sunscreen and a reusable water bottle.

Is it safe for young children?

Yes. All activities are supervised, and animals are gentle and used to visitors.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are not allowed on the farm to protect our animals and crops.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 7 days before your visit. After that, 50% is refundable.

Is the farm accessible for elderly guests?

Yes. Ground-floor rooms and a golf cart for field tours are available on request.
